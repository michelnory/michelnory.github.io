##### Why math?
Since I was a little I’ve always wanted to know how things work. When no one was watching I would destroy my toys and try to build them back or combine pieces here and there to make a different toy.

Throughout my studies all of the subjects related to science were my favourites, but when I had to make a decision for university I knew it had to be Mathematics.

Building rich networks of interconnected truths, hidden at plain sight, from a small set of axioms has shaped the way I solve problems: question assumptions, break problems into manageable parts, and search for connections that might not be obvious at first.

I want to carry this approach not only to the pure aspect of mathematics but to discover insights within data that help me solve problems for me and others.

##### Why Programming?
Programming came to my life quite naturally, it was the tool that enabled me to unleash my creativity (digitally at least).

Early on I learned Python because I wanted to build my own games, then I wanted to create an Android app so I had learn Java (Before Kotling was the industry defacto), after that I wanted to create a website so learning HTML, CSS and JavaScript was inevitable, etc
